The Bio

Boxing Neuro-Performance Coach

The evolution of an elite framework.

When Maria founded MAGOCLUB in 2019 in the heart of Marylebone, she set out to redefine the standard of Central London wellness. Over the last seven years, she built a reputation for designing high-fidelity training systems that respect both the complexity of modern high-performance and the architecture of long-term physical longevity. From precision-engineered personal strength coaching to the technical sanctuary of Couture Combat™, her work has always been rooted in giving clients absolute command over their bodies and minds.

But true high-performance never stands still. Driven by a lifelong obsession with three distinct pillars—the science of the mind, the clinical physiology of the body, and the deep, tactical discipline of boxing—Maria has evolved the MAGOCLUB methodology to its highest tier.

Today, she brings these forces together to operate as a specialized Boxing Neuro-Performance Coach. By shifting the focus to the central nervous system (CNS), she helps professional boxers and elite combat athletes balance their physiological budget, master autonomic recovery, and unlock extreme performance. This is not a departure from her origins; it is the ultimate realization of them.
